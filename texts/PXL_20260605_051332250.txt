Life is endowed with many chapters.
All part of a story that makes up one's life.
Seeking closure to certain chapters in one's life
can be quite challenging. To move forward from
the last chapter or seek a change in how the
story goes, can prove to be very difficult, but
not impossible!
The tumultuousness of life conjures many emotions.
Some with reprehensible regret.
Oh! But how to reconcile with ones being?
Particularly when clarity to adequately
review one's whole state of being, becomes
impaired due to extraneous forces beyond one's
physical, mental, emotional & spiritual control.
How to effectively solve little quandaries
requires many manipulations of mind, alliance,
occupation, interests etc...
We have facility to influence change and
be influenced to make change.
To learn from others, experience something
new every day, contributes to an ever changing
state of mind. After all, it is plasticity of mind
that allows for manipulation to occur.
The power of suggestion every day! It is one's own choice
what to do or not.
However, the winds of change that influence
life's choices can manifest yet another chapter.
So too, those same winds of change can
blow us into disarray. Only to start yet,
another chapter.
Andrew Kondas 2014
